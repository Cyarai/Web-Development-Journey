Pizza

Pizza is a universally loved Italian dish featuring a flat, round base of leavened wheat dough. It is traditionally topped with a savory tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and a customizable variety of meats, vegetables, and herbs. Baked at very high temperatures, it offers a satisfying contrast between a crispy yet chewy crust and a rich, gooey topping.

How to Make a Pizza

  1. Prepare the Dough: Stretch or roll out your pizza dough on a floured surface until it is flat and round.
  2. Add the Sauce: Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border at the edges for the crust.
  3. Add Cheese: Sprinkle a generous layer of shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  4. Add Toppings: Place your favorite ingredients, such as pepperoni, sliced mushrooms, or bell peppers, on top of the cheese.
  5. Bake: Bake in a preheated oven at 450°F (230°C) for 10 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
