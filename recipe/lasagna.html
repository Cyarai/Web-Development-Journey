Lasagna

Lasagna is a popular baked pasta dish made with flat, wide sheets of pasta stacked in layers. Cooked layers alternate with savory meat or tomato sauce, creamy cheese, and rich white sauce.

Prepare Ingredients

  1. Cook meat: Brown ground beef or sausage in a skillet.
  2. Make sauce: Add crushed tomatoes, garlic, and herbs to the meat.
  3. Simmer sauce: Let it bubble on low heat to deepen flavors.
  4. Mix cheeses: Stir ricotta, egg, and parmesan in a bowl.
  5. Boil noodles: Cook pasta sheets until soft if using standard noodles.

Assemble Layers

  1. Base sauce: Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  2. First pasta: Lay noodles side-by-side over the sauce.
  3. Cheese blend: Spread a layer of the ricotta mixture over the noodles.
  4. Melty cheese: Sprinkle shredded mozzarella on top.
  5. Repeat process: Continue alternating sauce, noodles, and cheeses to the top.
  6. Final top: End with a heavy layer of mozzarella and parmesan cheese.

Bake and Serve

  1. Cover dish: Tent the pan with aluminum foil to prevent sticking.
  2. Initial bake: Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes with foil on.
  3. Brown top: Remove foil and bake 15 more minutes until bubbly.
  4. Rest meat: Let it sit for 15 minutes before cutting.
